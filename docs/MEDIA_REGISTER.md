All eight files are approved for inclusion subject to the linked license conditions. The BY-SA images and adaptations of those images remain available under their respective licenses. This licensing statement concerns the photographs and their adaptations, not unrelated application code. Keep public attribution accessible: title or caption, creator, source link, license link, and modification statement. Do not suggest photographer or manufacturer endorsement.

Processing: EXIF orientation applied, RGB conversion, proportional downscaling without upscaling, WebP quality 85 using Pillow 12.1.0. No car shape, paint, location, or lighting was generated or retouched. CSS presentation may crop or darken photographs; this is included in each public modification statement. Naming colors describes visible photographs; exact manufacturer paint codes have not been verified.

## Selection and remaining acquisition gaps

The orange front Revuelto has the strongest available composition and the smallest large-image transfer size; use it for the first hero. White/lime Revuelto images support an honest curated look gallery. The shots were taken at different events and angles, so they are not a continuous color configurator or a 360-degree rotation. Rear and cockpit views cover the engineering/interior narrative. Temerario has two views with different photographed finishes. Urus has one approved front image; do not invent a second angle.

No rights-cleared moving footage or continuous image sequence was found and acquired in this bounded research pass. A set of unrelated still photographs is not a video sequence. Use the approved poster/still experience and retain the sequence renderer for future licensed footage. Do not extract manufacturer or YouTube footage without explicit derivative rights. Planned footage and image-sequence performance budgets remain unmeasured.
