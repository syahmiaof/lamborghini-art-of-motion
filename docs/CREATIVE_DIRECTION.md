# Creative direction — darkroom → wind tunnel → atelier

## Governing idea

The car begins as a few illuminated cuts in darkness. The site progressively gives the visitor more information and more control: first silhouette, then speed, then engineering, then surface and cabin, then a calm opportunity to compare and configure. The mood shifts from **mystery → force → precision → intimacy**.

## Visual grammar

- **Composition:** gallery-scale photography, off-axis crops and disciplined negative space. Change the visual mass per chapter; do not repeat a hero plus text block eight times.
- **Typography:** condensed display face for large chapter statements; restrained grotesk for navigation/specification; compact monospaced numerals only where data needs alignment. Final font choice requires license and rendered tests.
- **Color:** near-black/charcoal foundation; warm off-white information; one controlled signal color drawn from the approved hero car's paint or brake light. Avoid generic neon gradients.
- **Graphic material:** thin measuring lines, large chapter numerals, cropped technical labels, editorial captions. Speed marks should reveal real data, not decorate empty space.
- **Pacing:** slow first two viewports, compressed acceleration in velocity, regular cadence for facts, breathing room in interior and closing.
- **Sound:** off by default. A sound vignette is optional only with cleared audio and explicit control; never autoplay.

## Layout signatures

| Act | Page geometry | Type/image relationship | Motion discipline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Darkroom | One lit edge in a large black field | Short title sits away from the car; no paragraph over the headlight | One reveal, then hold. |
| Wind tunnel | Diagonal crop and precise horizontal metric rail | Numbers sit in clear, quiet cells beside imagery, never over a busy body panel | Faster masks; verified data arrives at the moment it is visible. |
| Atelier | Large detail crop with a narrow, well-spaced caption column | Small factual copy and human-scale reading width | Almost still; one restrained image transition. |

Use this as an art-direction test, not as three visual templates. Each chapter still needs its own composition. The exact accent color, typefaces and image crops remain contingent on the approved photography.

## Three signature frames

1. **Awakening:** 70–80% darkness, one headlight/light signature, small chapter index, restrained title. A still frame must work even before JavaScript loads.
2. **Velocity:** vehicle crosses a masked split composition; speed/acceleration figures arrive when their corresponding movement becomes legible. Avoid fabricated telemetry.
3. **Atelier:** close material detail and a quiet side column of factual interior notes. Slower, no perpetual parallax.

## What makes it recognizably this project

The visual system follows a car being *revealed, measured and considered* rather than an abstract luxury template. Key transitions take their direction from the vehicle's silhouette, lighting and engineering story. If a motion beat cannot be explained in terms of the content it reveals, remove it.

## Reject during review

- A centered headline plus symmetrical car render repeated in every chapter.
- HUD marks that look technical but encode no real information.
- A “cinematic” intro that prevents a visitor from reading or skipping.
- Empty luxury adjectives or copy that sounds like a manufacturer quote.
- Heavy smooth scrolling that harms wheel, keyboard or touch control.

## Copy tone

Short and physically specific. Proposed original lines such as “Before the road, a pulse” are concept copy, **not** Lamborghini quotes or technical claims. Product labels and specification units stay plain and sourced.
