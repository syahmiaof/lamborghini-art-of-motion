# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User preference: React, TypeScript, Vite, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, optional Lenis. CSS modules or SCSS for composition; 3D only if justified by assets and performance.

## Users

Primary: recruiters, creative technologists, design engineers and potential collaborators evaluating the founder's frontend, visual and motion craft. Secondary: automotive enthusiasts exploring an independent concept.

## Product Purpose

A frontend-only portfolio exhibit showing high-quality scroll storytelling, motion engineering, responsive UX and credible automotive content. Success means a visitor can understand the concept, explore the chapters, interact with the model showcase and identify the creator's work.

## Positioning

A chapter-driven digital exhibition built around the transition from awakening to speed to craftsmanship; its portfolio value is the combined visual, interaction and engineering execution.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One primary storytelling page with accessible chapter navigation, model comparison and a visual-only configuration concept.
- Real model names and claims require source and retrieval date. No invented price or purchase flow.
- Footer must state: “Independent concept experience. Not affiliated with Automobili Lamborghini.”
- Actual photos, video, music, trademarks and derived frames require the right to use before publication. The brief currently supplies no licensed media.
- Frontend-only: no backend, account, checkout or actual dealer enquiry.

## Brand Commitments

Working title: “LAMBORGHINI — THE ART OF MOTION.” The visual work must clearly identify itself as independent rather than posing as Lamborghini's official site.

## Evidence on Hand

- User's pasted project brief, 11 October 2026.
- Official model pages and Lamborghini terms reviewed 11 October 2026; URLs and verified facts are in `docs/research/MODEL_FACTS.md`.
- No approved photography, video, audio, logo license or regional official price is presently in this folder.

## Product Principles

1. Let genuine vehicle imagery and grounded model facts lead.
2. Make each scroll transformation explain a new part of the vehicle or story.
3. Keep comparison and configuration interactions honest about what they do.
4. Preserve the story on short screens, touch devices and reduced-motion settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable navigation and controls, semantic text separate from canvas, useful image alternatives, visible focus, adequate contrast and an equivalent reduced-motion reading path are required for the planned build.
